# Hook Scorecard

Scoring: factual anchor, specificity, tension, relevance, promise accuracy; 0–2 each.

| Candidate | Approach | Scores | Total | Decision |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| `X is retiring Creator Revenue Sharing on 7 September. Its replacement still pays for impressions.` | contradiction | 2/2/2/2/2 | 10 | selected |
| `In April, X said 100 stolen reposts a day were crowding out creators.` | concrete moment | 2/2/2/2/1 | 9 | strong, but starts with attributed rhetoric |
| `A creator can now lose not only reach but eligibility when X decides the contribution is too small.` | consequence | 1/2/2/2/1 | 8 | too broad before enforcement data exists |
| `The repost economy did not disappear when X renamed the payout program.` | answer first | 0/1/2/2/0 | 5 | rejected; rollout had not begun |

Selected opening maps to C01 and C02 and immediately exposes the central tension: the meter survives while eligibility changes.
