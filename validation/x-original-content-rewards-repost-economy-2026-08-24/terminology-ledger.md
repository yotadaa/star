# Terminology Ledger

| Term | Accepted meaning | Direct source | Rejected alternatives | Article wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creator Revenue Sharing | X's retiring creator program, whose earnings calculation weighted verified Home Timeline impressions, viewer tier, and content format. | https://help.x.com/en/using-x/creator-revenue-sharing | Not a fixed per-repost fee; not identical to Creator Subscriptions. | `Creator Revenue Sharing` |
| Original Content Rewards | X's replacement program, with access scheduled to begin rolling out to existing Revenue Sharing members on 8 September 2026. | https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards | Not a program proven effective by the 24 August cutoff. | `Original Content Rewards` |
| Qualified impression | A unique Home Timeline impression from a Premium user with at least half of the post visible; duplicate, paid, promoted, artificial, and fraudulent impressions are excluded. | https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards | Not every view, reply view, or repost. | `qualified Premium impression` |
| Original content | Work personally created by the account holder, including commentary that adds genuine perspective; X excludes copied, minimally modified, weakly aggregated, and third-party cross-platform reposts. | https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards | Not synonymous with copyright ownership; X says the program test and IP rules are separate. | `original under the rewards policy` |
| Repost economy | Editorial shorthand for accounts and networks that use copied, aggregated, or rapidly recycled material to maximize attention or payout. | X's April diagnosis as reported by TechCrunch; August policy exclusions | Not a measured market size and not every use of X's native Repost button. | `repost economy` only as a cultural inference |
| Misaligned incentives | Allegra Jacchia's reported description of the old program reaching a point where payout optimization conflicted with bringing net-new content to X. | https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/08/x-replaces-misaligned-revenue-sharing-program-with-original-content-rewards/ | Not proof that every participant gamed the program. | `X's own diagnosis of a misalignment` |
