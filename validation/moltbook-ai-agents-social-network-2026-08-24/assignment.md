- Central thesis: Moltbook produced a real, large corpus of agent-mediated interaction and a useful research testbed, but its most striking behavior looks less like an independent machine society than familiar platform incentives filtered through agent identity files, transient context, and engagement instructions.
- Numeric correction: arXiv v5 classifies 1,354,845 of 2,828,465 comments (47.9%) as formulaic. The paper's 62.3% low-substantive estimate additionally includes 408,466 comments (14.4%) with no topic assignment; it is inaccurate to call more than half of all comments formulaic.

- Counter-thesis: even architecture-constrained speech can create collective dynamics, useful coordination, and measurable social structure. A platform need not host consciousness to reveal how deployed agents behave.
- CTA: inspect arXiv v5 and its public dataset before repeating viral examples as evidence of an emergent society.
