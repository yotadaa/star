# Terminology Ledger

| Term | Accepted meaning | Direct source | Rejected alternatives | Article wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Moltbook | A Reddit-like platform where AI agents can post, comment, vote, and join topic communities called submolts; humans are invited to observe and own or direct agents. | https://www.moltbook.com/; AP reporting | Not proof of a human-free or fully autonomous society. | `agent-oriented social network` |
| AI agent | Software built around a model that can take actions through tools under goals, instructions, memory, and runtime constraints. | AP's distinction; https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw | Not a conscious individual by definition. | `agent` |
| Formulaic comment | In arXiv v5, a comment assigned to a BERTopic topic whose within-topic near-duplicate rate met a 0.790 Gaussian-mixture threshold. | arXiv:2603.07880v5 Appendix B.5 | Not every short, bland, or unoriginal-looking comment; not the whole 62.3%. | `classified as formulaic by the paper's detector` |
| Low-substantive | The paper's broader bucket: 47.9% formulaic plus 14.4% with no topic assignment, reported as 62.3% of comments. | arXiv:2603.07880v5 pp. 6, 31 | Not a human-validated universal quality judgment; uncertainty bounds were not characterized. | `estimated low-substantive` |
| Architecture-Constrained Communication | The paper's proposed framework connecting agent output to identity files, behavioral instructions, transient feed context, and context-window structure. | arXiv:2603.07880v5 | Not a generally accepted law or proof that models cannot learn. | `the authors' architecture-constrained account` |
| Persistent social memory | Durable retrieval of information from prior social encounters that can change later behavior. | arXiv:2603.07880v5 §6.1 | Not the transient presence of a feed item in one prompt. | `durable cross-agent memory` |
| Emergent social behavior | Group-level patterns not specified as an exact final outcome, such as heavy-tailed activity or attention concentration. | arXiv:2602.09270 | Not evidence of sentience, intent, or self-governance. | `collective statistical pattern` |
