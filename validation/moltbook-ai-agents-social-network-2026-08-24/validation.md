# Validation

## Editorial result

- Thesis: Moltbook generated real large-scale interaction and familiar attention patterns, but visible social behavior does not by itself prove durable peer learning or independent machine motives.
- Numerical boundary: 1,354,845 of 2,828,465 comments are reported in the v5 analysis as formulaic, or 47.9%. The separate 14.4% unassigned-topic group is discussed only when explaining the authors' broader 62.3% low-substantive estimate.
- Authorship boundary: agent posts can still depend on human-set prompts, schedules, model selection, tools, identity, and goals.
- Architecture boundary: short-horizon contextual adaptation is distinguished from persistent memory and durable learning.
- Benefits covered: a shared field site for agent interaction research, visible system-level repetition, and practical signals for feed, identity, memory, and moderation design.
- Costs covered: prompt injection and credential risk, cheap high-volume output, concentration around hubs, attribution ambiguity, and the risk of generalizing one platform configuration to all agents.
- Ending: answers the “bots invented slop” hook with an operational yes and an authorship/cognition qualification, then points to the paper and dataset. There is no generic research-note or source-cutoff paragraph.

## Evidence gate

- Seven used sources were opened and captured: the v5 primary paper, Moltbook, the public dataset repository, OpenClaw, two independent primary research preprints, and AP reporting.
- Eight screenshot files cover those seven sources, including the bounded v5 paper-results crop.
- Exact corpus: 47,379 agent accounts, 361,605 posts, 2,828,465 comments, 100 largest submolts, and a 27 January–18 February collection window.
- Network findings: about 7.8 comments per post, 94.7% depth-one comments, a 0.942 interaction Gini coefficient, and 76% of interactions captured by the top 1%, all attributed to the v5 paper.
- Method limit: the formulaic threshold was not presented with held-out category precision/recall and uncertainty bounds. The article does not convert the estimate into a universal quality score.
- Dataset limit: the repository's current longitudinal subset is not treated as identical to the frozen paper corpus or as a full platform export.
- All URLs, versions, limits, and capture filenames are recorded in `source-ledger.md`; all numeric and interpretive claims are mapped in `claim-ledger.md`.
