# Terminology Ledger

| Term | Accepted meaning | Direct source | Rejected alternatives | Article wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `AI info` | Meta's contextual label for content whose signals or disclosure indicate AI generation or modification; since September 2024, Meta says labels for detected editing may sit in the post menu while detected generation remains visible. | [Meta policy](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/) | A verdict that a model made the whole work; proof of deception. | `AI info label`, followed by what Meta says triggered it. |
| `Made with AI` | Meta's earlier label, renamed in July 2024 after it acknowledged that minor AI-assisted retouching could trigger wording that exceeded audience expectations. | [Meta policy](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/) | A current universal label; an exact measurement of AI contribution. | Used only for the 2024 chronology. |
| Industry-standard indicator | Metadata or another shared technical signal that a tool includes in media and that Meta reads when assigning a label. | [Meta policy](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/) | Visual intuition alone; a universal AI-content classifier. | `provenance signal` or `industry-shared indicator`. |
| Content Credentials / C2PA | Tamper-evident provenance assertions attached to supported media; they describe origin and edits when present but can be absent after a screenshot or other workflow. | [Content Authenticity Initiative FAQ](https://opensource.contentauthenticity.org/docs/getting-started/faqs/) | A truth score; an indelible watermark; proof that every uncredentialed image is fake. | `Content Credentials metadata`. |
| False positive | A platform label applied to work that the creator says was human-made, or a label whose visible wording overstates a minor AI-assisted edit. | I01–I05 | Proof that no AI-affiliated tool touched the file; deliberate platform defamation. | `reported mislabel` for unverified creator cases; `label mismatch` where an AI edit exists but the badge collapses its extent. |
| `AI Creator` | A separate 2026 Instagram profile-level self-identification feature described as optional in named reporting. | [Digital Camera World](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/tech/social-media/this-optional-instagram-ai-creator-label-is-a-step-in-the-right-direction-but-i-think-it-should-be-enforced) | The same control as a post's `AI info` label. | Mentioned only to distinguish profile identity from post provenance. |
| Reputational cost | Creator concern that audiences or brands may read an AI label as an accusation of synthetic authorship. | [Business Insider Taiwan](https://www.businessinsider.tw/article/5559) | A measured revenue loss in every case. | `reported reputational and contractual risk`; no fabricated loss figure. |
