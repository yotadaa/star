# Feature art direction

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Asset type: wide editorial Blog feature image.

Primary request: a top-down documentary still life about choosing between two deployment paths for one web application.

Scene: a worn plywood workbench with one unbranded kraft project folder at the bottom center. Two hand-built routing assemblies sit above it. On the left, many small paper asset cards bypass a tiny brass timing gate and fan directly into a broad distribution tray. On the right, every card enters a larger padded black equipment case with roomy memory blocks and a single orderly cable path. Thin black cotton cords connect the folder to both assemblies.

Medium and texture: practical prop photography, cut paper, plywood grain, scratched anodized metal, cotton cord, pencil marks without readable words, slight glue residue, natural manufacturing imperfections.

Composition: 16:9 landscape, overhead camera, clear left-versus-right geometry, quiet negative space around the assemblies, no central split line, no symmetry polished to perfection.

Lighting and palette: soft window light from upper left; charcoal, kraft paper, muted rust, dull silver, faded cream; restrained contrast and visible surface texture.

Constraints: no logos, no provider colors as branding, no legible text, no numbers, no fake interface, no screens, no server-room cliché, no people, no hands, no glossy 3D render, no glass cards, no neon, no gradients, no floating icons, no futuristic glow, no watermark.
