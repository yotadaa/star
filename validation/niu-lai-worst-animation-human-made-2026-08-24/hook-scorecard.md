# Hook Scorecard

Scoring: 0–2 each for factual anchoring, specificity, tension, reader relevance, and promise accuracy.

| “Mockery made *Niu Lai* visible. The reported story of two people working for five years changed what that mockery meant.” | Answer first | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 10 | **Selected.** Short, defensible, and states both cause and later reframing without collapsing them. |
| “Cinemas that received almost no promotional material began drawing their own posters after strangers turned a crude animation into a group outing.” | Consequence | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 9 | Vivid, but delays the article's central anti-AI boundary. |

## Selected opening

“Mockery made *Niu Lai* visible. The reported story of two people working for five years changed what that mockery meant.”

The next sentences will give AP's time-stamped box-office reversal and its inability to verify the production account independently. This prevents the title and hook from presenting a reported family story as settled fact.
