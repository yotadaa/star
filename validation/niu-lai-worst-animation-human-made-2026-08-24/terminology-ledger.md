# Terminology Ledger

| Term | Accepted meaning | Direct source | Rejected alternatives | Article wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Niu Lai* / 牛来 | The film's romanized Chinese title; AP reports the literal wordplay as “the bull is coming,” while other English coverage renders it as “The Cow Is Coming.” | AP report, lines 1789–1800 in the opened source; Guardian, 17 Aug 2026 | One fixed official English release title was not established. | *Niu Lai* throughout; explain the wordplay once without presenting a definitive English title. |
| “Worst animation ever” | A viral label used by commentary and headlines, not a measured ranking or official award. | Creative Bloq headline and audience-reaction reporting; Guardian criticism | An objective all-time ranking. | Put the phrase in quotation marks and attribute it to viral reception. |
| Two-person production | Public credit/trade reporting identifies Xin Yumeng and Sun Lifang/Sun Lifan as the central credited creators; sources vary on transliteration and AP could not verify the family account independently. | Red Star Capital republication, 15 Aug 2026; AP, 18 Aug 2026; Guardian, 17 Aug 2026 | A verified claim that no distributor, administrator, or outside contributor touched the release. | “reported two-person creative team” or “the two names in public credits.” |
| Human-made / handcrafted | A provenance claim that the production relied on human labor rather than generative AI; it does not certify artistic merit or technical competence. | First Financial, 17 Aug 2026; Guardian, 17 Aug 2026 | A synonym for good, ethical, or professional. | “reported human-made production” and “visible labor.” |
| AI slop | A pejorative audience label for AI-assisted output perceived as generic, repetitive, or low effort; it is a cultural judgment, not a stable technical category. | Reddit firsthand discussion; Guardian's attributed Douban reaction | Any work that used an AI tool. | Use sparingly and only when describing audience discourse. |
| Box-office snapshot | A time-specific total during a fast-moving theatrical run, not the film's final gross. | AP, 18 Aug 2026; Red Star Capital, 15 Aug 2026 | A closing total or profitability figure. | Attach every amount to its reported date. |
