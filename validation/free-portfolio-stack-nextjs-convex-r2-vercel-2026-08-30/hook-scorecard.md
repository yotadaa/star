# Hook scorecard

Scoring: specificity, surprise, evidence value, reader utility, and fit, each from 0–2. A publishable hook needs at least 8/10.

| Candidate | Specificity | Surprise | Evidence | Utility | Fit | Total | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| “The cheapest portfolio stack is not one free service. It is three different definitions of free.” | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 10 | Selected idea; rewritten into the opening answer. |
| “A Next.js portfolio can run for nothing until one upload turns storage into billing.” | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 9 | Strong but overstates the likelihood of a charge. |
| “Vercel, Convex, and R2 all say free, but each one stops being free differently.” | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 10 | Used as the second-sentence turn. |
| “The right free portfolio stack depends on whether the portfolio is actually an application.” | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 7 | Better as the conclusion. |

Selected hook: lead with the different meanings of `$0`, then answer the stack question before discussing implementation.
