- Candidate thesis: Next.js on Vercel Hobby, Convex Free, and Cloudflare R2 Standard can keep a content-rich personal portfolio's infrastructure at $0 while usage stays inside each provider's current allowance, but the stack has five separate boundaries: Vercel's non-commercial Hobby rule, Convex hard caps, R2 checkout and billable overages, a production custom domain whose registration or renewal is external, and the operational cost of joining three services.
