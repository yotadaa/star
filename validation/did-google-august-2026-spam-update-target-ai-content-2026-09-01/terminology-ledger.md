# Terminology ledger

| Term | Meaning used | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Spam update | A significant improvement to Google's automated spam-detection systems. | It does not disclose every changed signal or policy. |
| Scaled content abuse | Many pages created mainly to manipulate rankings rather than help users, regardless of whether people, automation, or both produced them. | AI use alone is not the violation. |
| SpamBrain | Google's AI-based spam-prevention system. | An AI detector does not prove that AI-authored pages were the special target. |
| Manual action | A Search Console-visible action resulting from human review. | A clean report does not exclude algorithmic evaluation. |
| SERP volatility | Measured movement in search-result positions. | It measures disruption, not motive or classifier. |
