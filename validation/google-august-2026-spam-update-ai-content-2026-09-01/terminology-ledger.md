# Terminology ledger

| Term | Meaning used in the article | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Spam update | A significant improvement to Google's automated spam-detection systems. | It does not disclose every changed signal or policy. |
| Scaled content abuse | Many pages created mainly to manipulate rankings rather than help users, regardless of whether automation, humans, or both produced them. | AI use alone is not the violation. |
| SpamBrain | Google's AI-based spam-prevention system. | An AI detection system is not evidence that AI-authored pages were the rollout's special target. |
| Manual action | A Search Console-visible action resulting from human review. | A clean report does not rule out algorithmic changes. |
| Algorithmic evaluation | Automated systems assessing pages and sites. | It is distinct from a manual action and has no reconsideration-request shortcut. |
| Volatility | Measured movement in search-result positions. | It measures change, not the motive or classifier behind it. |
| Rollout | The period in which Google deploys an update. | Temporal overlap does not prove causation for an individual site. |
