## Feature art direction and inspection

- Visual lessons combined: DeepSeek's official page gives modules equal visual weight; its architecture document places the model among replaceable runtime parts; Codex's safety account treats boundaries and approvals as execution machinery rather than prompt prose; the benchmark abstracts separate model and harness as measured variables.
- Direction: tactile architectural cutaway, small faceted model core, large surrounding system, muted rust and teal on near-black, one amber signal, matte materials, no shiny product-page treatment.
- Exclusions: no logos, people, fake interface, source documents, quotations, labels, benchmark values, or watermark.
- Full-size inspection: passed. The image contains no legible text, brand mark, faux evidence, person, or metric. The square and triangular tokens inside the sandbox read as abstract objects, not a real UI. The 1672 × 941 crop leaves the core and each main subsystem visible at article-card and wide-header ratios.
- Final prompt: recorded in the task transcript and summarized by the direction above; built-in image generation was used.
