# Hook scorecard

Scoring: 0–2 each for factual anchoring, concrete specificity, real tension, reader relevance, and promise accuracy. An 8/10 threshold decides which hook can enter the draft.

| H01 | Contradiction | Coding agents are still sold under model names, yet a 2026 controlled study found up to a 40× difference in tokens per solved task after changing the harness while keeping the model fixed. | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 10 | Selected. | H02 | Concrete moment | DeepSeek opened Harness v0.1 on August 13, 2026, with four words that move the model out of the center: “Everything is a plugin.” | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 9 | Strong section opener, but the interpretive phrase “move the model out of the center” needs immediate qualification. |
| H03 | Consequence | A stronger model can cost more, wait longer, or fail in the same old loop when its execution system presents the wrong tools and feedback. | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 7 | Rejected as the lead. It lacks a named study and mixes several outcomes before giving the reader a measured anchor. |
| H04 | Answer first | The harness can matter more than the model once the decision concerns cost, action boundaries, recovery, and auditability rather than an isolated answer. | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 9 | Used as the promise sentence after H01. It defines “more important” without pretending the model effect disappeared. |

## Selected opening

H01 followed by H04. The next sentences introduce DeepSeek's “Everything is a plugin” claim and state the uncertainty boundary before any broader interpretation.

## CTA test

The earned action is a fixed-model comparison with shared tasks, budgets, and validators, followed by inspection of the DeepSeek architecture. This action follows S04 and S05 rather than asking for generic engagement.
