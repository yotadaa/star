# Hook scorecard

Scale: 0–2 per criterion. An 8/10 total is the editorial threshold.

| Candidate | Hook | Factual anchor | Specificity | Tension | Relevance | Promise accuracy | Total |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A — contradiction | By the afternoon of 24 August, the strangest part of Xiaomi's new phone chip was no longer its name. XRING O3 was official; its 5.22 million AnTuTu result was still Xiaomi's result. | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | **10** |
| B — concrete moment | Xiaomi's verified phone account published one compact launch sheet on 24 August: ten CPU cores, a new 16-core GPU, LPDDR6, and 200 TOPS. It supplied no retail device test beside those numbers. | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | **10** |
| C — consequence | XRING O3 gives Xiaomi more room to shape cameras, local AI, memory, and power around its own devices, yet the chip still sits on Arm IP and an outside foundry. Control has expanded; dependence has changed shape. | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | **10** |
| D — answer first | XRING O3 is real, and the absent O2 is no longer the useful question. The useful question is whether Xiaomi's second flagship chip can turn launch-day gains into sustained results in a shipping phone. | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | **10** |

## Selection

Candidate A opens the article. It settles the naming issue immediately, introduces the difference between fact and claim, and prepares the reader for a pro/con review without promising an O3 benchmark verdict that cannot yet exist.

Candidate C supplies the thesis line after the evidence table. Candidate D informs the resolution.
