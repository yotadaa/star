## Art direction and review

- Subject: one unbranded mobile SoC package between a generic foldable-device form and a wafer tool.
- Composition: wide 16:9 workbench, with copper traces connecting design, device, and fabrication.
- Medium: restrained editorial 3D illustration with matte ceramic, brushed metal, and wafer textures.
- Palette and light: charcoal and oxidized copper under cool industrial light.
- Exclusions: logos, model names, numbers, benchmarks, fake user interfaces, fake documents, recognizable products, people, and foundry branding.
- Inspection result: passed at full image and article crop. No legible text, logo, watermark, fabricated metric, or claimed real-world scene appears. The foldable form and wafer equipment remain generic.
