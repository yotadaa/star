## Thesis

XRING O3 is an official, strategically meaningful public successor to Xiaomi's O1 flagship SoC, but its launch proves control and continuity more clearly than it proves a performance crown: every O3 speed and efficiency number available at the cutoff comes from Xiaomi, while the supply chain still runs through licensed IP, an outside foundry, and an undisclosed modem arrangement.

## Boundary

No independent O3 retail-device test was available by the research cutoff, Xiaomi did not explain the skipped O2 label, and the opened record does not establish O3's foundry contract, modem, global availability, yield, unit cost, or sustained performance as first-party fact.
