## Social-search boundary

- X redirected the exact-phrase search for `"XRING O3"` to its login flow. Screenshot: `sources/S13-x-search-access.png`.
- Threads returned no public results for the same exact phrase in the logged-out surface. Screenshot: `sources/S14-threads-search-access.png`.
- No indexed X or Threads post carries a material claim in the article. Weibo supplied the accessible first-party social record.
- The Omdia PDF found during discovery was excluded after the in-app browser blocked its URL, so no uncaptured Omdia claim appears in the draft.

## Research Gate result

**Pass, with a hard boundary.** Xiaomi's own post and same-day reporting settle the product name and announcement. They do not settle retail performance, sustained efficiency, modem behavior, final device availability, or software quality. O1 measurements can test whether Xiaomi has prior credibility, but they cannot stand in for O3 measurements.
