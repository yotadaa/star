# Terminology ledger

Research cutoff: 24 August 2026, 23:59 Asia/Jakarta.

| Term | Accepted meaning | Direct source | Rejected or unresolved alternatives | Article wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| XRING O3 | Xiaomi's officially announced public successor to the O1 flagship smartphone SoC. Xiaomi's verified phone account used the Latin label `XRING O3` on 24 August 2026. | [Xiaomi phone Weibo post](https://m.weibo.cn/status/5335442573754887); screenshot `sources/S15-xiaomi-weibo-post.jpg` | It is no longer a rumor or typo. The earlier `O2` label did not become the public product name. | `XRING O3` |
| Missing O2 | A gap in Xiaomi's public flagship naming between O1 and O3. | The official O3 post names O3; the 2025 official announcement names O1. | No opened Xiaomi source explains why O2 was skipped. Any explanation would be speculation. | `Xiaomi skipped the public O2 label without explaining why` |
| 玄戒 O3 | The Chinese name rendered as XRING O3 in English-language reporting and Xiaomi's prior global XRING branding. | Xiaomi Weibo; [Xiaomi global O1 announcement](https://www.mi.com/global/discover/article?hl=en-GB&id=4926) | Do not translate 玄戒 literally or treat it as a separate chip family. | `XRING O3` |
| SoC | System-on-chip: a component combining CPU, graphics, AI, imaging, and related functions. | [Reuters, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/xiaomi-launches-new-xring-chip-partners-with-tsmc-production-sources-say-4872712) | It does not automatically mean that every radio, memory, or power component is integrated. | `mobile system-on-chip (SoC)` at first use |
| Self-developed | Xiaomi led the SoC and system design. It does not mean Xiaomi owns every CPU/GPU building block or fabricates the silicon. | [Arm on XRING O1](https://newsroom.arm.com/blog/xiaomi-xring-o1-silicon); Reuters | `Fully independent`, `fully domestic`, and `vertically integrated fabrication` are unsupported. | `Xiaomi-designed` or `in-house design` |
| 3 nm | A foundry process-family label, not a literal measurement of one transistor feature and not a performance guarantee. | Xiaomi's launch claim; Reuters' sourcing on TSMC; [TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260430-13028.html) | Xiaomi's first-party post did not name the foundry or process variant. Reuters attributed TSMC manufacturing to unnamed sources. | `3 nm, according to Xiaomi; TSMC-made, according to Reuters` |
| TOPS | Trillions of operations per second, a vendor-stated peak throughput measure whose precision and supported data types matter. | Xiaomi Weibo post | It cannot be compared cleanly across products without test conditions and numeric formats. | `a claimed 200 TOPS` |
| LPDDR6 | A mobile memory standard supported by XRING O3 according to Xiaomi. | Xiaomi Weibo post | Support does not establish retail memory capacity, sustained bandwidth, latency, or battery impact. | `LPDDR6 support` |
| SME2 | Arm Scalable Matrix Extension 2 instructions aimed at matrix-heavy work. Xiaomi says the CPU includes two SME2 acceleration units. | Xiaomi Weibo; [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/xiaomi-xring-03-3702037/) | It is not a substitute for an application-level AI benchmark. | `two SME2 acceleration units, according to Xiaomi` |
| All in AI / AI flagship SoC | Xiaomi's launch positioning for O3. | Xiaomi Weibo post | Treat as marketing language, not an independently measured category. | Attribute to Xiaomi or omit |
| O3 modem | The cellular modem arrangement in an O3 shipping phone. | No opened first-party or independent source disclosed it by the cutoff. | O1's external modem cannot be carried forward as an O3 fact. | `undisclosed at the cutoff` |
