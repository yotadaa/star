## Selected opening

“A Salt Lake City venue asked promoters to hire a local artist, cut up magazines, or ask for help. It would no longer share AI flyers.”

The following paragraph will explain that this is one named example, not evidence of a universal price premium, before connecting it to platform controls, polling, and authorship badges.
