# Hook scorecard

Scale: 1–5 for specificity, honest curiosity, factual integrity, natural voice,
and ability to carry the reader into the next paragraph.

| Hook | Specificity | Curiosity | Integrity | Voice | Transition | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **A.** At 2:00 AM in Jakarta on 26 August, one X post named an alleged OpenAI pretrain called Bel. Five hours later, the claim had become a roadmap, a giant star, and a conclusion about Anthropic. | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **25** |
| **B.** OpenAI has confirmed Astra and real training workloads at Stargate. It has not confirmed Bel, Doug, a 10-trillion-parameter model, or the line connecting them. | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 5 | **23** |
| **C.** The most revealing part of the Bel rumor is not its codename. It is how “more than 10 trillion total parameters” became the softer phrase “10T+ pretrain” as the post travelled. | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **23** |
| **D.** One social post supplied Bel, Doug, a GPT-6 lineage, an AGI threshold, and Anthropic's alleged private posture. The public record supplies Astra and a great deal of missing connective tissue. | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **22** |

## Selected hook

Hook A. It begins with a verified timestamp and observable propagation, contains
no promise that Bel exists, and naturally opens the origin investigation.
