# Claim ledger

## Thesis and boundary

**Thesis:** Pay-to-rank sites became a fast, measurable clone wave because the purchase, status contest, and public screenshot are the same action, but the evidence says most copies sell novelty rather than durable ad performance; the original captured most reported money, while the variants with clearer turnover, disclosure, moderation, and measurement address only part of that weakness.

**Boundary:** The evidence can establish public mechanics, visible social amplification, a directory's indexed corpus, and board- or founder-reported receipts. It cannot establish global trend status, audited revenue, net proceeds, advertiser conversions, or the full universe of deleted, private, and unindexed projects.
