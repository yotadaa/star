# Terminology ledger

Research cutoff: 2026-08-24, Asia/Jakarta (UTC+7).

| Term | Accepted meaning | Direct source | Rejected alternatives or limits | Article wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pay-to-rank board | A public list in which a payment amount, cumulative paid total, or paid balance determines placement. | [Topple rules](https://topple.lol/rules); [TopBid](https://www.topbids.lol/); [outoutbid method](https://outoutbid.lol/api) | It is not one standardized auction format. Individual sites add decay, quality scores, fixed sponsor slots, or timed prizes. | pay-to-rank board |
| Bid | The amount a participant pays or adds to a listing to change its position. On several inspected sites it is a final one-time payment, not a refundable offer awaiting auction close. | [Topple rules](https://topple.lol/rules); [TopBid](https://www.topbids.lol/) | Avoid implying that every site runs a legally or economically formal auction. | bid, with the site's payment rule stated nearby |
| Ad slot / paid placement | A page position sold for money, whether the operator calls it an ad, sponsor slot, leaderboard position, or takeover. | [FTC paid-placement letter](https://www.ftc.gov/sites/default/files/documents/closing_letters/commercial-alert-response-letter/commercialalertletter.pdf); [FlappyBid rules](https://flappybid.lol/rules) | “No ads” is a slogan used by some boards to reject ad networks or algorithms; it does not erase the paid-placement character of a purchased rank. | paid placement or sponsor slot |
| Leaderboard takeover | A time-bounded purchase that gives one advertiser a larger area or an entire page rather than only one row. | [TopBid](https://www.topbids.lol/) | Not present on every board and not equivalent to permanent first place. | masthead takeover |
| Quality multiplier | Topple's site-specific factor, up to 2x, that raises standing and reduces click drain for listings clicked more often than the board median. | [Topple rules](https://topple.lol/rules) | It is not Google's Quality Score and carries no third-party audit. | Topple's click-based quality multiplier |
| Conversion | A business action after an ad interaction, such as a purchase, sign-up, call, or download. | [Google Ads conversion definition](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6308?hl=en) | A recorded outbound click is not a conversion. | conversion |
| Merchant of record | A seller of record that handles transaction and tax obligations for a supplier. | [Polar Merchant of Record documentation](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/introduction) | The public record shows a Polar webhook during Outbid's launch; it does not establish the current payment relationship. | payment provider or merchant of record, only when the exact role matters |
| “Trending” | A platform-level claim that normally needs a disclosed trend ranking, time series, or comparable measurement. | No qualifying X or Threads trend dataset was found. | Raw post views, a clone directory, and several creator posts show attention within a searchable indie-builder slice, not a global platform trend. | Omitted. Use “fast clone wave in the indexed indie-builder web.” |
| Revenue | Money an operator says a site has taken. | [Outbid creator's 24-hour post](https://x.com/jonathan_wilke/status/2090548427616555154); [outoutbid methodology](https://outoutbid.lol/api) | Public counters do not establish net revenue, refunds, taxes, fees, chargebacks, or audited recognition. | founder-reported receipts or board-reported takings |
| Permanence | The duration for which a paid listing or winning placement remains visible. | [Topple rules](https://topple.lol/rules); [FlappyBid rules](https://flappybid.lol/rules); [TopBid](https://www.topbids.lol/) | Some rows persist, some balances decay, and some prizes last one day. | state the exact rule instead of “permanent” as a category claim |
