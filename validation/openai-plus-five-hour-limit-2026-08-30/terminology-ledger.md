## Unresolved wording

The announcement said the five-hour limit would remain disabled for the $100
and $200 Pro subscriptions for the upcoming months. The current pricing table
nevertheless expresses Pro capacity as estimated messages per five-hour
window. The checked sources do not explain how the table relates to that
promised exemption, so the draft does not assert a current Pro enforcement
state.
