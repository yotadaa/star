# Hook scorecard

Scoring uses the grounded-blog heuristic: 0–2 each for factual anchoring,
specificity, tension, relevance, and promise accuracy.

| “The return protects a weekly allowance by making part of it temporarily unavailable.” | contradiction | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 8 | rejected; compresses variable mechanics too aggressively |
| “A Plus subscriber can still have weekly capacity left and be stopped by the shorter window.” | consequence | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 8 | rejected as universal; account behavior and active-turn policy remain contested |

## Selected opening

The final opening combines the first candidate's dated reversal with the second
candidate's bounded answer. It states the decision immediately, then corrects
the common wall-clock interpretation without overstating account-specific
capacity.
