## Evidence and editorial gates

- Every package must contain an assignment, terminology ledger, claim ledger, source ledger, visual ledger, hook scorecard, Markdown draft, native payload, source captures, assets, and validation log.
- Research must reach primary artifacts, first-party statements, named original reporting, relevant papers or datasets, and attributable firsthand posts where available.
- Every used source must be opened and preserved as an exact-page Browser capture.
- Disputed cultural explanations remain synthesis unless direct evidence supports causation or prevalence.
- Drafting starts only after the central thesis, boundary, counterevidence, names, dates, figures, and terminology pass the Research Gate.
- Articles use warm third-person English, meaningful headings, direct links beside claims, explicit benefits and costs, and a source-led CTA.
- No article ends with a generic source-cutoff, social-search, methodology, or `Research note` paragraph. Evidence limits sit beside the claims they constrain.

## Visual direction

- Each article contains one bounded, attributed real source-evidence capture selected from its opened sources.
- Each article also has one original feature image with no factual burden.
- Feature images avoid glossy 3D, cinematic product still lifes, glowing circuitry, fake UI, fake headlines, fake code, excessive symmetry, and generic blue-orange lighting.
- Preferred language: editorial paste-up, risograph, halftone, cut paper, stamped index cards, contact sheets, visible fibers, restrained inks, imperfect registration, and deliberate negative space.
- Collected web images remain private references. Only generated originals and rights-reviewed bounded evidence captures enter payloads.
- Every encoded file needs measured dimensions, a SHA-256 checksum, durable asset key, useful alt text, and a caption that advances the article.
