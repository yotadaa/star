# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, collaborators, faculty, community organizers, and technical peers who want to quickly understand Mukhtada Billah NST's work across fullstack web, AI tooling, data science, research, and community projects. They arrive in a browsing context, not a transactional workflow, and should leave with a clear sense of capability, taste, and trajectory.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Mukhtada as a builder-researcher through a game-like "quest journey" metaphor. Success means visitors can scan the story, inspect proof of work, move between projects/research/contact routes confidently, and feel that the gamification is a real interaction layer rather than decorative labels.

## Brand Personality

Warm, mechanical, exploratory.

The brand should feel like a personal RPG cockpit for a builder: tactile, slightly pixelated, research-aware, and grounded in real projects. It should stay polished and legible rather than becoming noisy or novelty-heavy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI SaaS gradients, purple-blue glow systems, fake dashboard metrics, static game labels without feedback, overused glass cards, emoji-based gamification, and template-like repeated uppercase section scaffolding. Do not add fabricated achievements, fake repo numbers, fake citations, or decorative game mechanics that imply data the portfolio does not actually have.

## Design Principles

1. Turn labels into systems: if the page says quest, tier, journey, or achievement, give it a visible state, progress, or feedback loop.
2. Preserve identity before novelty: Fraunces, Silkscreen, Nunito, Verdant Dusk colors, and hard-offset cards remain the core language.
3. Use real proof as the reward: project screenshots, GitHub activity, publications, and timeline milestones carry the visual weight.
4. Make motion tactile but quiet: press states, unlock reveals, pulses, and ripples should feel responsive without interrupting reading.
5. Keep the world accessible: keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion fallbacks, and no horizontal overflow are non-negotiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for text contrast and keyboard access. All motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Gamification must not rely on color alone; labels, accessible names, and semantic structure should preserve meaning. Touch devices should avoid desktop-only cursor or hover requirements.
